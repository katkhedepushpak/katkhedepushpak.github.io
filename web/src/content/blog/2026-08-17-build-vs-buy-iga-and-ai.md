The "Build vs. Buy" debate is getting more interesting with AI coding tools like Claude entering the picture.

As a software engineer working with an IAM team, I've started to see a different side of this debate in higher education.

The integration landscape itself can become so complicated that we end up outsourcing business logic into custom scripts and automation just to achieve some of the fundamentals that an IGA platform doesn't provide out of the box.

You might have HR, SIS, LMS, research systems, directories, legacy applications, federated identity, and highly customized access requirements — all connected through different workflows and data models.

At that point, the question isn't simply: *"Should we build or buy an IGA platform?"*

It becomes: **"How much are we actually building around the platform?"**

If the answer is a growing collection of scripts, integrations, workarounds, and custom logic, the economics of "buy" start looking very different.

This is where AI-assisted development tools like Claude get interesting. They can potentially make it significantly easier to build, test, and maintain the custom layer required to bridge those gaps.

The future may not be Build vs. Buy.

It could be:

- **Buy** the foundation.
- **Build** the missing pieces.
- **Use AI** to make the custom layer sustainable.
